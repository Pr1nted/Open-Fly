Open Fly now plays Open Doctrines v1.2.1a

2026-09-15

Open Doctrines v1.2.1a is out, and the simulated fruit fly brain -- the FlyWire connectome, 138,639 neurons -- is playing it now, in the browser.

What the fly did

As Free Republic of Sweden in 1914 (seed 20260801), the fly started with 1.05% of the world and was wiped out on turn 52, after 39 turns at war. It gave 270 orders, from an average of 401 descending-neuron spikes a turn.

This is the first version the fly has played, so there is nothing to compare it with yet.
